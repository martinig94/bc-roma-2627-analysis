Rome gets its first Serie A basketball derby since 1980 tonight: Maxima Roma vs BC Roma, PalaEur, 18:15.

I spent the week building a small open pipeline on the legabasket.it play-by-play (every shot with coordinates, every assist linked to its basket, every substitution) and the EuroCup box scores, to see what four games per team already tell us. A few things that stood out:

🏀 Two opposite offences. BC Roma lives at the rim: 38 points per game inside 4.5 m, 82% in the restricted area, 18/23 on drives. Maxima scores 25 inside (13 below league average), shoots 52% at the rim and survives on the free-throw line: 26 attempts a game at 82.5%.

🏀 Shared vs concentrated. Maxima uses 12 players, nobody above 25 minutes, 45% of its points from the bench, three scorers within three points of each other. BC Roma rides seven players for 85% of the minutes, and one connection carries the offence: Mannion-to-Simonovic, 9 of Mannion's 17 assists.

🏀 The pattern to watch. Maxima has lost a third or fourth quarter badly in three of its four games (12–26, 21–33, 12–32). BC Roma's own weakness is fouling: 28.5 opponent free throws per game, against the team that draws more fouls than anyone in the league.

🏀 Kelly Olynyk, signed last week, is not registered in time and misses the derby.

One honest caveat: this is built only on the box scores and the play-by-play. It tells you where shots come from and who is on the floor, not how they were created, so nothing here is a comment on defensive schemes, pick-and-roll coverages or set plays. That needs the film.

My read from the numbers: BC Roma controls the paint and the glass and wins by a handful in a high-80s game; Maxima's route is the line, the bench and the start of the second half.
