### [Derby della Capitale, by the numbers](/about_me/Derby_Roma_2026.html)
Data preview of Maxima Roma – BC Roma (LBA 2026-27), built on the league play-by-play and the EuroCup box scores. The pipeline behind it, [bc-roma-2627-analysis](https://github.com/martinig94/bc-roma-2627-analysis), collects every LBA game weekly and regenerates game, team, matchup and player reports in one command.
