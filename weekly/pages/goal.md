# Goal

The goal is to develop tools that improve the work of **scouting and game preparation** of BC Roma.

The idea is to build a seamless process that saves the staff's time and turns the available data into valuable insights. The amount of
data in a season is far larger than anyone can read: games of two competitions, box scores, play-by-play, shot locations, lineups. The
role of this tool is to act as a **filter and a sense-maker**: it collects and organises the data automatically, brings forward what is
worth attention, and leaves to the staff the part that no model can do, which is to read the game, decide and prepare the team. It
supports the decision-making process of the staff in game preparation; it does not replace it.

## Why data analysis matters in sport

- Numbers give a **common, checkable starting point**: what a team actually does (how it shoots, where from, how it scores, who plays
  together) can be compared across games, opponents and competitions, instead of relying on memory and impressions of a few games.
- Analytics is most useful when it is **combined with the eye of the coach**. Data points to where to look, video and experience say
  why it happens and what to do about it. In basketball, shot selection, pace, efficiency and lineups are the kind of questions that
  data answers well and quickly.
- Doing the repetitive part automatically **frees time** for the analysis that needs a person, and makes the preparation of every game
  follow the same method, so that games and opponents are comparable week after week.

## How it works

The idea is to use data and technology to optimise the workflow and improve performance through a **continuous process of feedback and
ideas**. Each analysis is generated automatically from the data, then reviewed and completed by the analyst; comments and suggestions
from the staff are collected on every page and fold back into the next version of the tools.
