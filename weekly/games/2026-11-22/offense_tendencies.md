# Offense tendencies: Virtus Bologna

## Key points

- ==Bologna rarely loses the ball== (second-lowest turnover rate in the league): [turnovers](#turnovers).
- ==It runs==: transition is 18% of its possessions, 2nd in the LBA, and efficient: [play types](#play-types-and-sets).
- ==Cuts and handoffs are its most efficient half-court plays== (4th in the LBA each): [play types](#play-types-and-sets).
- ==Carr is the creator==: [assists](#assists).
- ==Same play mix in the Euroleague, far fewer points== (transition 2nd in share in both, 4th in efficiency in the LBA and 18th of 20 in the Euroleague): [play types](#play-types-and-sets).

## Play types and sets

- Spot-ups (21%) and pick-and-roll ball handlers (20%) are the main play types; transition is 18% (2nd highest share in the LBA, 4th in points per possession).
- Cuts are the most efficient play (4th in the league; Diarra and Klintman take most of them); handoffs and post-ups are also 4th.
- Weak spots: roll man and off-screen plays, both 13th of 16 in points per possession.
- Put-backs are rare (4% of possessions, last in the league).

- The mix is the same in the two competitions: spot-ups, pick-and-roll ball handlers and transition are about 60% of the possessions in both, and transition is the 2nd highest share in each.
- The efficiency is not: the ball handler, transition, isolations, post-ups and handoffs all score much less in the Euroleague. The roll man is the one play that scores clearly more there (Diarra, whose main play it is: 1.27 points per possession over his 123 Euroleague possessions).
- Put-backs are more frequent in the Euroleague (6% of the possessions, 5th highest share) than in the LBA (4%, last).
