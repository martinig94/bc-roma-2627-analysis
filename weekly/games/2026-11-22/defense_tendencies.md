# Defense tendencies: Virtus Bologna

## Key points

Synergy, 8 Serie A and 13 Euroleague games (pulled 20 Nov 2026); ranks within each competition.

- ==Man-to-man, no traps==: [defensive scheme](#defensive-scheme).
- ==The pick-and-roll ball handler and isolations are held down in both competitions==: [what they allow](#what-they-allow).
- ==Put-backs and post-ups are where opponents score==: [what they allow](#what-they-allow).
- ==Spot-ups: 2nd best defended in Serie A, 17th in the Euroleague==: [what they allow](#what-they-allow).
- Where to look on film: [clips](#clips).

## Defensive scheme

Tendencies from Synergy's tags, to confirm on film.

- **Man-to-man**: zone on 3% of the half-court possessions.
- **Pick-and-roll**: almost never trapped (4 times in 724); the ball handler loses the ball on 18% of his possessions.
- **Isolations go at the big men** (Diarra, Klintman, Diouf): consistent with switching.
- **Post-ups are doubled about one time in three**; the pass out of the double pays (16/28 from three).

- Press on 9% of the possessions: opponents lose the ball on 20% of them.

## What they allow

- **Few points**: 3rd in Serie A, 7th in the Euroleague in points allowed per possession.
- **Best defended**: pick-and-roll ball handler, isolations, cuts, shots at the rim (57% made).
- **Weakest**: put-backs (14th in both competitions) and post-ups.
- **Different by competition**: spot-ups (2nd in Serie A, 17th in the Euroleague).

- Transition is rare (12% of the possessions) but costly.

- Catch-and-shoot jumpers tagged as guarded go in at 39% (15th in both); threes allowed 34% in Serie A, 37% in the Euroleague.
