# Defense tendencies: Virtus Bologna

## Key points

Synergy, pulled on 20 Nov 2026: 8 Serie A games (ranks over the 16 teams) and 13 Euroleague games (ranks over the 20 teams), the games of 17 and 19 Nov included.

- ==Man-to-man almost always==: zone on 3% of the half-court possessions: [defensive scheme](#defensive-scheme).
- ==The pick-and-roll ball handler is held down in both competitions== (4th best in Serie A, 3rd in the Euroleague): [defensive scheme](#defensive-scheme).
- ==Opponents isolate against Bologna's big men==, and score little: [defensive scheme](#defensive-scheme).
- ==Put-backs and post-ups are where opponents score in both competitions==: [what they allow](#what-they-allow).
- ==Spot-ups are 2nd best defended in Serie A and 17th in the Euroleague==: [what they allow](#what-they-allow).
- Where to look on film: [clips](#clips).

## Defensive scheme

Read from Synergy's situation tags over the 21 games; coverages are tendencies to confirm on film.

- **Man-to-man.** Zone on 54 of 1,676 half-court possessions (3%): 14 in Serie A (9 of them against Verona), 40 in the Euroleague.
- **Pick-and-roll: no traps, help that recovers.** 4 traps in 724 pick-and-roll possessions; the ball handler scores 0.83 points per possession in both competitions and loses the ball on 18% of them.
- **Isolations go at the big men.** Serie A opponents isolate against Bologna more than against any other team, most often against Diarra, Klintman and Diouf (54 of 134 isolations): a pattern consistent with switching. They score 0.75 points per possession.
- **Post-ups are doubled about one time in three**, and the pass out of the double pays: 16/28 from three.

- Zone: 0.89 points per possession on those 54 possessions (20/41 from the field), too few to rank.
- Press: 164 possessions (9% of all; 8th highest share in Serie A, 10th in the Euroleague), almost always followed by a half-court possession (152). Opponents score as against the rest of the defense (0.93) but lose the ball more (20% against 13% overall). Most pressed: Bayern (18 possessions) and Trieste (17).

### Pick-and-roll

- Including the passes out of it, the pick-and-roll is 38% of what Bologna defends (724 possessions): 0.94 points per possession, 3rd in Serie A and 7th in the Euroleague.
- The defense commits to the ball (help comes and the ball is passed) on 415 of them (57%): 0.91 points per possession and a turnover on 20%. In Serie A that is 0.81 (3rd), in the Euroleague 0.98 (11th).
- When the ball handler finishes himself: 375 possessions, 39% from the field (104/267). Dribbling off the pick 313 possessions (0.81), going away from the pick 54 (0.87).
- Roll man: 140 possessions, 1.04 points per possession (9th in Serie A, 4th in the Euroleague).

### Isolations and post-ups

- Isolations: 134 possessions, 39/115 from the field. 3rd best in Serie A (0.73), 8th in the Euroleague (0.77). When the defense commits to the isolation the pass out is costly (44 possessions, 1.27, 11/19 from three): small sample.
- Post-ups: 119 possessions, 0.96 points per possession (11th in Serie A, 15th in the Euroleague), and nobody in the Euroleague is posted up more often. Including passes 165 possessions: the defense commits on 58 (1.14 points per possession), 29 of them hard doubles (1.21).

### Game situations

- **Long possessions.** 1 possession in 5 against Bologna ends in the last 4 seconds of the shot clock: the highest share in Serie A and the 2nd in the Euroleague. Opponents shoot 38% there (125/327).
- **Transition is rare but costly.** 12% of the possessions (13th highest share of 16 in Serie A, 17th of 20 in the Euroleague) for 1.15 points per possession, with free throws on 19% of them.
- **After time-outs**: 240 possessions, 0.85 points per possession (2nd in Serie A, 8th in the Euroleague). Baseline out of bounds 0.66 on 110 possessions (2nd and 3rd). Sideline out of bounds: 0.77 in Serie A (5th), 1.07 in the Euroleague (19th).
- **By quarter**: Serie A 0.93 / 0.92 / 0.97 / 0.89 points per possession, with a turnover forced on 20% of the fourth-quarter possessions; Euroleague 1.01 / 0.92 / 0.98 / 1.03.

## What they allow

- **Few points in both competitions**: 0.93 per possession in Serie A (3rd), 0.99 in the Euroleague (7th); few free throws in both (5th lowest rate).
- **Best defended in both**: the pick-and-roll ball handler, isolations, cuts and shots at the rim (57% made, best in the Euroleague, 3rd in Serie A).
- **Weakest in both**: put-backs (14th in each, 61% made) and post-ups; catch-and-shoot jumpers tagged as guarded go in at 39%.
- **Different by competition**: spot-ups (2nd in Serie A, 17th in the Euroleague), turnovers forced (15% and 5th, 12% and 19th) and defensive rebounds (71% and 5th, 67% and 17th).
