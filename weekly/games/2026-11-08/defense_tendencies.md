## What they allow

Play types and shot zones allowed, points per possession, rebounds and turnovers forced.

- Strongest: P&R roll man (48 possessions, 0.708 PPP, 1st in the LBA on 1 Nov; 20.8% turnovers) and isolation (21, 0.476; 3/18 from the field).
- Weakest: cuts (43 possessions, 1.186 PPP, 63.6% FG, 21 of 33 shots made) and put-backs (33, 1.273, 65.4% FG).
- Spot-ups are 28% of what Trieste defends, the highest share in the LBA on 1 Nov (with Trento).
