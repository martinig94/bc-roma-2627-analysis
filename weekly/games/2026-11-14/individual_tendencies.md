# Individual tendencies: Verona

## Key points

- ==Tobey: the most efficient scorer==, 17 points a game, almost all at the rim, and the team's offensive rebounder.
- ==Samuels: the most possessions, below-average efficiency==; many free throws and turnovers.
- ==Loro: the shooter==, 7 of 10 shots are threes.
- ==Zampini and Andrews: the ball handlers==; Andrews has struggled to score (30% from the field).
- ==Massinburg==: 13 points a game in the first four rounds, out since.
