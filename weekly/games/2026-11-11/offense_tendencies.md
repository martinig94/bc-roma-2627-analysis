# Offense tendencies: Ulm

## Key points

- ==Spot-ups and transition== are the two largest play types, about 41% of the possessions; see [play types](#play-types-and-sets).
- The ==put-back and the cut== are the most efficient plays per possession; see [play types](#play-types-and-sets).
- The ==P&R ball handler is the weak spot==: low PPP and a quarter of the possessions lost; see [play types](#play-types-and-sets) and [turnovers](#turnovers).
- Watson and Atamna are the main passers, ==Heckel the most assists per minute==; see [assists](#assists).

## Play types and sets

Main play types, pace, first options, sets after timeouts and out-of-bounds plays.

- Spot-up: 124 possessions (23.5%), 1.121 PPP, 58.6% eFG, 72% of the shots threes, 6.5% turnovers (4th of 32 on 5 Nov).
- Transition: 94 possessions (17.8%), 1.074 PPP, 67.7% eFG, 20.2% turnovers (25th on 5 Nov).
- P&R ball handler: 72 possessions (13.6%), 0.847 PPP, 26.4% turnovers (20th on 5 Nov).
- Sets after timeouts and out-of-bounds plays are not in the data (needs film).
