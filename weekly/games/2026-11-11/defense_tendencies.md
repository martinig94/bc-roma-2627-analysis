# Defense tendencies: Ulm

## Key points

- ==Best EuroCup defense against the P&R ball handler== and off-screen plays; see [what they allow](#what-they-allow).
- ==Weak in transition== and against spot-ups and isolations; see [what they allow](#what-they-allow).
- ==Forces turnovers== on 17.6% of possessions; see [what they allow](#what-they-allow).
- Coverage and scheme cannot be read from the data; see [defensive scheme](#defensive-scheme).

## Defensive scheme

Pressure, pick-and-roll coverage, switching, zone use, transition defense.

- Not in the data: the pick-and-roll coverage, switching and zone use need film.
- What the numbers say: P&R ball-handler possessions end in a turnover 29.8% of the time and in a score 29.8% (104 possessions faced).
- Transition is the exception: 82 possessions faced, 1.390 PPP, 78.7% eFG.

## What they allow

Play types and shot zones allowed, points per possession, rebounds and turnovers forced.

- Strong: P&R ball handler 0.625 PPP, 35.6% eFG (1st of 32 on 5 Nov); off screen 0.583 on 12 possessions (1st); post-up 0.625 (4th); put-backs 0.923 (5th).
- Weak: transition 1.390 (29th), spot-up 1.015 (26th), isolation 1.000 (26th), with cut (1.220, 15th) and roll man (1.032, 14th) in the middle.
- Zones allowed are not in the pull (it holds Ulm's own shots only).
