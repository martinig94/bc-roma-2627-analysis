Methodology

How the numbers on this site are produced, so that any figure can be checked and every limit is visible. Nothing here is a judgement about plays or tactics: the pages are built from box scores and play-by-play, then reviewed and completed by hand.

The data cut: only what was known before the game

Every pre-game page (the opponent analysis and the BC Roma snapshot) uses only games played before the date of that game, in both competitions when the team plays both. A page about a game still to come is therefore regenerated whenever new games enter the data, and a banner says so when earlier games are missing (still to be played, or played and not collected yet). The post-game review uses the data up to and including its game.

Teams that play the EuroCup (BC Roma, Maxima Roma, and any opponent present in the EuroCup file) are shown with league and EuroCup games; the others with league games only. The tables say which competitions were included.

Definitions

  • Possessions = FGA − offensive rebounds + turnovers + 0.44 × FTA.
  • Offensive rating = 100 × points / possessions; defensive rating = 100 × points allowed / opponent possessions; net rating = their difference.
  • Pace = possessions per 40 minutes, the average of the team's and the opponent's possessions.
  • eFG% = (2P made + 1.5 × 3P made) / FGA. TS% = points / (2 × (FGA + 0.44 × FTA)).
  • TOV% = turnovers per 100 possessions. OREB% = offensive rebounds / (offensive rebounds + opponent defensive rebounds); DREB% is the mirror. FT rate = FTA / FGA.
  • PIR (valutazione) is the efficiency rating published by the league in the box score, not recomputed.
  • Per-game averages of a player count the games in which he played at least a second.
  • Opponent eFG% and forced TOV% are what the team allows its opponents.

Standings

Two points per win. Teams level on points are ordered by the games among them (wins, then fewest losses, repeated for any sub-group still level), then by overall point difference, then by points scored. Early in the season most tied teams have not met yet, so the order inside a group is provisional and may differ from the official table. For a EuroCup game the table is the EuroCup group of BC Roma as of the date of the game, computed from the EuroCup results: a group is the set of teams connected through the full schedule, and the same ordering rules apply (the official EuroCup tie-breaks may differ slightly). An LBA table is never shown for a EuroCup game.

Sections of the snapshot and of the analysis

  • Form: result of every game of both competitions, in date order.
  • Playing-time flag: in the last game, a player is flagged if he played at least 8 minutes and either 7 minutes more or 30% more than his average in the earlier games he played (both competitions). A player with no earlier minutes is shown as new.
  • Roster: per-game averages before the game; shooting as percentage with attempts per game in brackets.
  • Assist matrix: each assist event of the LBA play-by-play is linked to the field goal it set up; the matrix shows passer (rows) and scorer (columns), for players involved in at least four assisted baskets. Box-score assists are used as a check (the share covered is printed in the title). There is no play-by-play for EuroCup games here, so the matrix is LBA only.
  • Shot zones: shot coordinates (percent of the court) are converted to centimetres and folded onto one half court; nine zones (under the basket, short left and right, mid-range left, centre and right, three-point left, centre and right). FG% by zone is compared with all other LBA teams.
  • TS% vs PIR: players with at least 10 minutes per game; dot size is minutes per game; one plot with LBA games only and one with EuroCup games only. The grey dots are other teams' players in the same competition (in the EuroCup, only the teams present in the file).
  • Opponent comparison: the table puts BC Roma and the opponent side by side with the definitions above; the figures of the analysis (scoring sources, hot spots, key players, lineups) come from the same data cut.
  • Five-man lineups: rebuilt from the substitutions in the play-by-play; best and worst units need a minimum of minutes together and are ranked by net points per 40 minutes.

Page status flags

Pages are first generated automatically and then reviewed. For a game still to come, a banner at the top says Automatic draft, not reviewed yet (no text written), Work in progress (some text), or nothing once the notes file carries reviewed: yes. Other banners say which games before this one are not in the data yet.

Limits to keep in mind

  • Small samples. Early in the season a team has three to six games: percentages on few attempts are noise. Read tendencies, not conclusions.
  • Unofficial sources. The league API is not documented and can change; stats can be corrected after the game, and a stored game is not re-fetched unless it is deleted and collected again.
  • EuroCup coverage. Box scores exist only for the teams entered in the EuroCup file; home and away of those games are read from the order of the teams in the feed. Standings are computed here, not copied from the official table.
  • No tactical content in the automatic parts. Plays, coverages and intentions are for the written text of each page.
